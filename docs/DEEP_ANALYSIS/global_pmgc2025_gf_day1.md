# GLOBAL ANALYSIS — PMGC 2025 Grand Finals Day 1

> Event: PUBG Mobile Global Championship 2025, Grand Finals Day 1 — Dec 12, 2025,
> Siam Paragon, Bangkok, Thailand. English broadcast feed.
> VOD: 6.68 h (400.9 min), 640×360. 6 matches: M1 Rondo, M2–M4 Erangel, M5–M6 Miramar.
> $3,000,000 prize pool. 16 teams.
> Method: frame sampling (1 fps in 2-min windows every 30 min + 1/30 s sweep of hours 0–2),
> full-res spot frames, whisper transcription of 13× 3-min audio clips across the broadcast.
> Honest limits at the end.

## 1. Show structure (the full-day ritual)

**Pre-show ≈ 135 min (t 0–135).** Far longer and more produced than a typical match day:
| Time | Segment |
|---|---|
| 0–30 | Countdown + infinix sponsor ads, player booth cams, Thai-language interview packages with English subtitles |
| ~5 | Human-interest featurette (player video-calling his mom before the finals) |
| ~30–40 | English desk opens: host + analysts **Max** ("Maxman") and **Seven** — championship predictions, backstage stories |
| ~45–70 | Stage performances (Thai artists), 16 team walkouts with national flags |
| ~75 | Cinematic player-profile films |
| ~95 | **Creative Vision Awards 2025** ceremony embedded in pre-show (World of Wonder creator awards, presented by James Yang, Senior Director Global Esports, Level Infinite) |
| ~110 | **2026 season roadmap announcement**: PMGO 2026 Season 1 in Pakistan ($500K), PMWC 2026 at Esports World Cup Riyadh ($3M) |
| ~125 | Desk → casters handoff ("take it on over to our casters, John Allen and Blake") |
| ~135 | Match 1 (Rondo) flight path begins |

**Per-match cycle** (fast turnaround, ~2–4 min between matches):
flight path → gameplay → "WINNER WINNER CHICKEN DINNER" → winning-team stage celebration
→ **TOP 4 TEAMS** fullscreen graphic → bottom-strip match checklist (✓ on completed matches,
"00:34 NEXT MATCH" countdown) → next match flight path.

**Post-match graphics observed:**
- **TOP 4 TEAMS** (after M4, t≈303): player photos per team + ELIMS + **TRAVEL DIST** (e.g. 24.2 KM) —
  travel distance as a broadcast stat is distinctive.
- Bottom strip: RONDO M1 ✓ → ERANGEL M2 ✓ … with WWCD chicken icon on completed matches.

**End of day** (t≈392–400): on-stage winner interview (English host + translator) →
**WHOLE DAY HIGHLIGHT** replay montage → close.

**Breaks:** countdown + sponsor ads + map strip with completed-match checkmarks.

## 2. Gameplay overlay layout (exact, % of 640×360 frame)

- **Top-left** (x 0–17%, y 0–4%): "Remaining 51" | "Team 16" | "Time 9:52" — thin dark strip.
- **Killfeed** directly below top-left bar: 3–4 entries, "Killer [weapon] Victim" with country flags.
- **Top-center**: gold "PUBG MOBILE" logo.
- **Top-center team cards** (the signature element): 3–4 cards, each with **country flag + team
  abbreviation + alive-player bars + live stat row** (elims / knocks / etc., e.g. DX: 8-2-2-0).
  Shown for the leading/remaining teams; updates live.
- **Top-right**: minimap + zone stage + timer ("03:24 Stage 2").
- **Right panel** (x 83–100%, y ~15–90%): full 16-team standings, PERSISTENT during gameplay.
  Header: "ALIVE | PTS | FINISH". Rows: rank | logo+abbr | alive bars | elims.
  Blue rows = alive teams, red = eliminated. Footer tabs: "ALIVE | KNOCKED | ELIMINATED".
- **Bottom-left**: "PUBG MOBILE GLOBAL CHAMPIONSHIP | PUBG UNITED 2025" + "GRAND FINALS MATCH 1/18" + **infinix** sponsor.
- **Bottom-center**: observed player panel — team + player name, health, weapons; stat ranks.
- **Bottom-right**: facecam PiP (observed player, synced).
- **Center**: pure third-person observer view; team tags on map = colored pills with flags + abbreviations.

## 3. Map cam / tactical views

- Aerial/zoomed-out observer views used between fights and during rotations; team tags with
  national flags visible on terrain. (No separate "map cam mode" with facecam grid like India's
  2×2 — PMGC keeps the single facecam PiP.)
- Zone circle + stage + timer always on the minimap.

## 4. Camera switching (observed)

- Fights: observer **commits to one fight**; scope/ADS cams during engagements; vehicle chases held.
- All transitions observed are **hard cuts** (no fades/wipes).
- **No PiP of a second fight** during live play — same single-fight commitment as India.
- Facecam PiP syncs with observed player.

## 5. Replay

- **No instant replays during live matches** in the sampled segments.
- **"WHOLE DAY HIGHLIGHT"** montage package closes the broadcast (multi-minute frag reel with
  HIGHLIGHT bug) — this is the replay product, placed at day end, not between matches.

**Signature patterns:**
- **Roster/storyline depth**: "Guizão… probably going to be leaving the team after this";
  "Sylas wants that thing so dang bad" (2nd championship chase); Regnum Carya vs Dplus KIA
  "guys that took you down in the last PMGC" — grudges carried across years.
- **Rookie narratives**: Team Flash "rookie team not just of PMGC, possibly of the entire year".
- **National pride**: country flags on every team card; "I want to see that era of glory of DRX back to Korea".
- **Tactical vocabulary**: third-party theory, utility economy ("investing all the smokes"),
  "hit the 180 on both these squads", stutter-step/gyro analysis, zone-end prediction
  ("ends around zone eight instead of zone nine").
- **Playful banter**: "Secret agent Cody Banks" (sneaky solo), "yes chef Ramsay lemons"
  (grenade-cooking joke), "we can pretend… real imaginers" during flight phase,
  "you gotta trust me… I've watched this game a few hours".
- **MVP race built live**: "600 damage in the game for Doc… heading in the direction of a Doc MVP".
- **Points math on air**: "Vampire 25 points away from first… Regnum 18 behind"; "those 10 points to kick off PMGC would be tasty".
- Catchphrase: "knockout punch and a half", "slobber knocker" (Rondo), "crack on".

## 7. Graphics / animation quality

- Full 3D stage, LED walls, "2025 PMGC GRAND FINALS BANGKOK THAILAND" branding.
- Broadcast package: dark blue/gold premium theme; animated team cards; smooth lower-thirds.
- **PMGC CHAMPION PREDICTION** interactive graphic: in-game fan prediction event
  ("2025 PMGC CHAMPION SELECTION", "SELECTION REWARD", avatar redeem code) — game↔broadcast tie-in.
- Balenciaga / Porsche sponsor integrations (championship jackets, MVP Porsche Cayenne) — luxury-brand production value.

## 8. Pre-show / post-match production value

- ~135-min pre-show = a full entertainment show (concert, awards ceremony, season announcement),
  not just a countdown. This is the single biggest production gap vs regional broadcasts.
- Post-match is *fast*: celebration → Top-4 graphic → next match in ~2–4 min. No long desk breaks
  between matches; desk segments are short and narrative-driven.

## 9. Notable data features

- **Travel distance (KM)** as a team stat on the Top-4 graphic.
- Live team stat cards (elims/knocks) in the top bar for contending teams.
- Right panel: ALIVE / PTS / FINISH columns with KNOCKED/ELIMINATED tabs.

## 10. Honest limits

- 360p VOD (YouTube rip); overlay positions ±2%.
- 13× 3-min audio clips transcribed (~7% of audio); wpm figures are per-clip rates, not full-broadcast.
- Frame sampling: 1 fps in 2-min windows every 30 min + post-match windows; some segments (e.g. exact
  replay timing) inferred from windows.
- "No PiP / no mid-match replay" = not observed in sampled windows, not an exhaustive proof.
- English feed only; 21 other language feeds not analyzed.
