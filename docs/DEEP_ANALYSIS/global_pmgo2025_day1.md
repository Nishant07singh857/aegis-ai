# GLOBAL ANALYSIS — PMGO 2025 Main Event Day 1 (supplement)

> Event: PUBG Mobile Global Open 2025, Main Event Day 1 — Apr 12, 2025, Zo'r TV / Renaissance
> Hall, Tashkent, Uzbekistan. English feed. 12 matches under Smash Rule; Regnum Carya won
> (106 pts); MVP DOK (4Merical Vibes).
> VOD: 7.78 h. Method: 1-fps frame windows every 45 min (600 frames) + full-res spot frames
> + 4× 3-min audio clips transcribed (t 60, 150, 240, 330).

## Opening ceremony
Themed theatrical opener at Tashkent Railway motif: "we've seen many teams come and go…
we are approaching our final destination" — the train-station-as-journey metaphor, Smash Rule
explained, $500,000 prize pool, and the amateur-to-pro story ("90,000 amateur players competed
across more than 4,600 matches for the chance to be right here"). Smaller stage than PMGC but
the same ceremony ambition.

## Casters (English, different talent from PMGC)
- Style: analytical, rotation/zone-shift focused ("Very hard shift… over towards that northwest
  side", "2-2 split… right in the middle of it"), team-position tracking, scouting/information
  theory ("they have little to no information on the circle… some scouting, figure out the lay
  of the land").
- ~180–200 wpm in fights; same play-by-play + color commentator structure as PMGC.
- Less banter/gossip than PMGC's John Allen + Blake; more straight tactical calling.

## How PMGO differs from PMGC (same year, same publisher)

**PMGO runs a simpler, distinct broadcast package** — teal/cyan theme vs PMGC's blue/gold:
- Pre-show left panel: **SCHEDULE** list ("MATCH 1/12 @ TBD MAP", "MATCH 2/12 ERANGEL"…) with
  countdown, instead of PMGC's countdown + map strip.
- Smaller stage, studio-scale production (Zo'r TV hall) vs PMGC's arena.

**Gameplay overlay — same family, different skin, two notable feature differences:**
| Element | PMGO | PMGC |
|---|---|---|
| Top-left | "Remaining 9 \| Team 3 \| Time" + killfeed below | Same pattern |
| Top-center | **WWCD % win-probability cards** — 3 cards with flag + alive bars + "WWCD - 37%" (live predictive analytics for top remaining teams) | Live **team stat cards** (flag + abbr + alive bars + elims/knocks row) for 4 contending teams |
| Top-right | Minimap + zone stage + timer | Same |
| Right panel | **ELIMINATION RANKING** (match elim leaderboard: 1 GOAT 7, 2 AKE 6…) — header shows this is the *match* leaderboard, not overall standings | Full 16-team standings (ALIVE/PTS/FINISH) |
| Bottom-left | "PUBG MOBILE GLOBAL OPEN MAIN EVENT - DAY 1 MATCH 5 - ERANGEL" + esports logo | "GRAND FINALS MATCH n/18" + infinix |
| Bottom-center | Player panel: team + name + health/weapons + **AVG GAME stats (0.3, 67.0, 33.7)** | Player panel with stat ranks |
| Bottom | Facecam PiP (synced) | Same |

**The WWCD% prediction cards are PMGO's standout data feature** — a live win-probability model
surfaced directly in the top bar. PMGC (the bigger event) instead shows live team stat cards.
Neither exists in the India (BMSD) broadcast.

## What PMGO shares with PMGC (global constants)
- Right-side persistent team panel during gameplay; killfeed top-left; hard cuts only;
  facecam PiP synced; country flags on all team identification; English-language world feed.
