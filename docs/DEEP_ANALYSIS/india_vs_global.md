## 1. WHAT'S THE SAME (universal PUBG Mobile broadcast grammar)

These patterns hold across **all four broadcasts** — they are the genre's constants, not
any one producer's choice. AEGIS must treat them as non-negotiable:

1. **Persistent right-side team panel during gameplay.** India: x 84–100%, 16 teams, rank/logo/
   finishes/alive bars, "Stage N" + zone timer header, ALIVE/KNOCK/ELIM tabs. PMGC: same slot,
   rank/logo/alive/elims, blue=alive/red=eliminated, "ALIVE | PTS | FINISH" header. PMGO: same
   slot ("ELIMINATION RANKING"). **The panel NEVER disappears during gameplay in any broadcast.**
2. **Killfeed top-left**, 3–4 entries, "Killer [weapon] Victim", ~5 s fade.
3. **Top strip**: Remaining / Teams alive / match timer (+ map name, LIVE badge).
4. **Bottom-center observed-player panel**: name, health, weapons/ammo/boost (+ stat ranks).
5. **Facecam synced to observed player** — when the observer switches player, the facecam
   switches simultaneously.
6. **Hard cuts only.** Zero fades/wipes/dissolves observed in any of the four VODs. Every
   camera change is a 1-frame cut.
7. **Single-fight commitment.** All four broadcasts stay on ONE fight until it resolves
   (45–90 s holds measured in India; same behavior observed in PMGC/PMGO). **No PiP of a
   second live fight, no split-screen, anywhere.**
8. **No instant replay during a live fight.** Replays/highlights are packaged *after* the
   fact (PMGC's "WHOLE DAY HIGHLIGHT"; India's verbal recaps).
9. **WWCD ritual**: "WINNER WINNER CHICKEN DINNER" graphic + winner celebration + team stat
   cards after every match.
10. **Map cam between fights**: aerial/zoomed tactical view with team tags + zone circle +
    timer, 15–30 s holds, then hard cut back to action.
11. **Flight-path opener per match** and **countdown/break bumpers** between matches.

## 2. WHAT'S DIFFERENT

### 2a. Overlay layout (positions)
| Element | India (BMSD) | PMGC 2025 | PMGO 2025 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Team panel | Right, persistent | Right, persistent (same) | Right, persistent (same) |
| Killfeed | Top-left | Top-left (same) | Top-left (same) |
| Facecam | **Bottom-LEFT**, ~12% wide | **Bottom-RIGHT**, small PiP | **Bottom-center-left**, small PiP |
| Player panel | Bottom-center (x 35–65%) | Bottom-center (same) | Bottom-center (same) |
| Top-center | (empty / logo) | **Live team stat cards** — flag + abbr + alive bars + elims/knocks row for 3–4 contending teams | **WWCD% win-probability cards** — flag + alive bars + "WWCD - 37%" for top-3 remaining teams |
| Bottom-left | Facecam | Event branding + "GRAND FINALS MATCH n/18" + **infinix sponsor** | Event branding + "MATCH n/12 - ERANGEL" |
| Minimap | Top-right inset (map cam only) | Top-right always + zone stage/timer | Top-right always + zone stage/timer |
| National flags | Not present (all-Indian teams) | **On every team card, tag, and killfeed entry** | Same |

The skeletons are identical; the top-center is where the global broadcasts innovate
(India leaves it empty), and facecam/sponsor placement is mirrored.

### 2b. Information density & data features
- **India's edge in density**: standings shown after EVERY match (per-match + overall tables with
  ▲▼ movement arrows), plus FOUR leaderboard products (MVP rating ~1.0 scale, Eliminator,
  Best IGL, Finals MVP 3.2 scale) and team spotlight stats ("K9: 7002 damage, 31 knocks").
  Nobody else in this comparison does standings-with-movement-arrows after every single match.
- **Global's edge in live predictive/advanced stats**: PMGO's **WWCD% win probability**,
  PMGC's **travel distance (KM)** on the Top-4 graphic, live elims/knocks stat cards in the
  top bar, AVG GAME stats on the player panel. India has no predictive or per-player advanced
  stats on screen during gameplay.
- PMGC's right panel shows PTS + FINISH columns (tournament context live); India's shows
  finishes + alive bars (match context live).

### 2c. Camera work & fight coverage
- **Same philosophy** (commit to one fight, hard cuts, no PiP). No measurable difference in
  switching discipline between India and global.
- **Difference: observer anticipation.** PMGC's observer is consistently *on* the fight as it
  starts (scope cams pre-aimed, vehicle chases picked up early) — a staffing/skill gap more
  than a technical one. India's observer work is competent but the measured decision delay is
  ~2–3 s vs PMGC's near-instant fight acquisition.
- **Map cam**: India uses a dedicated map-cam mode (2×2 facecam grid, location labels like
  "Pochinki"). PMGC/PMGO integrate tactical zoom-outs more fluidly without a distinct mode
  switch — less "modal", more continuous.

### 2d. Replay
- **India (BMSD GF Day 1): no replay package aired at all** — only verbal caster recaps.
  (Biggest single gap in the India broadcast.)
- **PMGC: "WHOLE DAY HIGHLIGHT"** multi-minute frag montage closing the day + Top-4 team
  graphics with photos. Replays exist as *packaged* products, not instant mid-match inserts.

### 2e. Pacing & show structure
| | India (BMSD GF D1) | PMGC 2025 GF D1 |
|---|---|---|
| Total runtime | ~7 h | ~6.7 h |
| Pre-show | Bumper → Hot Seat → desk → cinematic opener + walkouts (long, ~60+ min incl. interviews) | **~135 min entertainment show**: concert, Creative Vision Awards ceremony, 2026 roadmap announcement, cinematics, walkouts |
| Between matches | Stats rundown → winner interview → break countdown → desk (long) | Winner celebration → Top-4 graphic → next flight path (**~2–4 min turnaround**) |
| Desk role | Heavy: standings tables after every match, 4 leaderboards | Light between matches; heavy in pre-show |
| End of day | (not documented) | Stage interview → WHOLE DAY HIGHLIGHT reel |

India spends its non-match time on **data** (tables, leaderboards); PMGC spends it on
**spectacle** (performances, awards, announcements) and keeps match-to-match turnaround tight.

### 2f. Caster patterns
| | India (Hindi/Hinglish) | PMGC (English: John Allen + Blake; desk: Max, Seven) |
|---|---|---|
| Speed | Fast, emotive | 160–227 wpm; fastest on desk (~227), 160–200 in fights |
| Structure | Storyline per table, gap highlights ("sirf 4-finish ka gap"), every segment ends on next flight path | Same narrative discipline: rookie arcs (Team Flash), grudge arcs (RC vs Dplus KIA across years), MVP race built live ("heading for a Doc MVP") |
| Signature | Hype peaks, crowd-energy calls | Tactical vocabulary (utility economy, third-party theory, "hit the 180", gyro/stutter-step analysis), zone-end prediction, playful banter ("Secret agent Cody Banks", "yes chef Ramsay lemons") |
| Info role | Points math, standings storytelling | Points math ("25 points away from first"), leaderboard awareness, **roster-move gossip** ("Guizão probably leaving after this") |
| Language advantage | Emotional immediacy for home crowd | **National-pride framing** (flags everywhere, "era of glory of DRX back to Korea"), cross-year storylines |

Both are excellent at narrative; India's is more emotive, PMGC's more analytical + gossip-driven.

### 2g. Graphics quality & animation
- **PMGC**: full 3D stage, LED walls, animated team cards, gold/blue premium theme, Balenciaga +
  Porsche luxury integrations, in-game **Champion Prediction** interactive event (redeem codes,
  avatar rewards — game↔broadcast tie-in). This is a tier above.
- **India**: clean, professional 2D package (checkered Krafton India backdrops, "TICKETS ARE LIVE"
  sponsor bars). Functional and polished, but a generation behind in motion design and
  interactivity.
- **PMGO**: simpler teal package — proof that "global" is not monolithic; the *event tier*
  sets the budget, and PMGC is the flagship.

### 2h. Pre-show / post-match production value
- PMGC's pre-show is a **~2 h variety show** (awards ceremony with Level Infinite's Senior
  Director presenting, season announcements, concerts). India's pre-show is broadcast-shaped
  (interviews, desk, opener). The gap is budget + ambition, not competence.

## 3. WHAT'S BETTER IN GLOBAL (AEGIS should adopt)

Prioritized by impact vs build cost:

**P0 — adopt now (cheap, high impact):**
1. **Top-center live team stat cards** (PMGC): flag + abbr + alive bars + elims/knocks for the
   3–4 contending teams. Uses data AEGIS already tracks. Fills India's empty top-center.
2. **WWCD% win probability** (PMGO): a simple live model (alive count + elims + zone position)
   surfaced as "WWCD - NN%" cards. AEGIS's Phase-13/14 data + heuristic engine can produce this
   honestly. *This is the single most "world #1" data feature in the comparison.*
3. **"WHOLE DAY HIGHLIGHT" reel**: package the day's frags into a closing montage. AEGIS
   already has replay + auto-highlights (Phase 9–11) — this is just the *placement*: end of
   day, not scattered.
4. **Match-strip checklist** (✓ on completed matches + "NEXT MATCH IN mm:ss" countdown):
   trivial, keeps viewers oriented across 6 matches.
5. **Travel distance (KM)** as a team stat — cheap from zone/position tracking, distinctive.

**P1 — adopt next (medium cost):**
6. **Faster match-to-match turnaround** (~2–4 min): celebration → Top-4 graphic → next flight
   path. India's long desk breaks between matches bleed momentum.
7. **National/regional flags on team IDs** for any multi-region event (AEGIS will run these
   when it goes beyond India).
8. **In-game interactive tie-ins** (Champion Prediction with redeem codes) — needs publisher
   cooperation, but it's the engagement mechanic India lacks entirely.
9. **Observer anticipation training**: PMGC's fight acquisition is near-instant. For AEGIS's
   AI director: cut to the fight *as it starts*, not 2–3 s later (killfeed/zone-event triggers).

**P2 — aspirational (needs budget/partners):**
10. **Pre-show as entertainment show** (performances, awards, season announcements) — this is
    what makes PMGC feel like a major event rather than a long stream.
11. **Luxury sponsor integration** done tastefully (Balenciaga jackets, Porsche for MVP).

## 4. WHAT'S BETTER IN INDIA (Krafton India does uniquely well)

1. **Standings discipline**: full per-match + overall tables with ▲▼ movement arrows after
   EVERY match. PMGC shows standings far less often. For a standings-obsessed audience this is
   genuinely better fan service — AEGIS should keep this, it's already in the templates.
2. **Leaderboard products**: four distinct boards (MVP rating, Eliminator, Best IGL, Finals MVP)
   with per-player granularity (damage, survival time, knocks). PMGC leans on narratives; India
   leans on numbers. Both work — India's is more *complete as a data product*.
3. **Caster-audience emotional sync**: the Hinglish desk's hype peaks and storyline-per-table
   rhythm ("sirf 4-finish ka gap") is perfectly tuned to its audience. PMGC's analytical style
   would feel cold to this crowd. Don't copy the tone — copy the *discipline*.
4. **Transparency under failure**: 2 voided + rehosted matches announced openly on broadcast.
   (No global equivalent observed to compare, but worth preserving as a value.)
5. **Team-spotlight stat drops** ("K9: 7002 damage, 31 knocks") — punchy, shareable, social-ready.

## 5. The one-line summary

**India wins on data discipline and emotional sync; global wins on spectacle, predictive data,
and pacing.** The universal grammar (right panel, killfeed, hard cuts, single-fight commit,
WWCD ritual) is identical — so AEGIS's foundation is already correct. The gap is everything
*around* the gameplay: what fills the top-center, what the breaks contain, how fast matches
turn around, and whether the broadcast *predicts* (WWCD%) or only *reports*.

## 6. Recommended AEGIS build order (from this comparison)

1. WWCD% live prediction cards (top-center) — new signature feature
2. Live team stat cards (top-center) for contending teams
3. Match-strip checklist + next-match countdown
4. Whole-day highlight reel placement (end of broadcast)
5. Travel-distance stat on team graphics
6. Tighter inter-match turnaround (director rundown change, not code)
7. Observer-anticipation tuning for the AI director (cut on fight-start triggers)
