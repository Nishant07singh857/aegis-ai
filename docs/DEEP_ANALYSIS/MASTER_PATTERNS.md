## 4. RESOLVED CONTRADICTIONS

### 4a. M18 WINNER — RESOLVED: Analyst B is correct ✅

**Claim:** Analyst A's Day-3 doc states "SOUL won Match 18 → champions."
**Correction:** **Version1 x Blue Army won Match 18** (Miramar, 8 finishes). Soul won the tournament via the **Smash Rule fallback** (no match-point team won a WWCD on Day 3, so the team with the most total points wins).

**Evidence (converging, three independent lines):**

1. **Three independent web sources agree:**
   - talkesport winner article: *"Version1 x Blue Army won Match 18, and SouL's lead held to the end"* (match table: M18 → Blue Army, 8 elims).
   - sportsdunia Day-3 live: *"Blue Army finished the tournament with a win. Soul wins BMSD 2026 at Hyderabad."*
   - sportsdigest: *"Blue Army won the Chicken Dinner in that match, but Team Soul's total points and Smash status secured the overall BMSD 2026 title."*

2. **Internal arithmetic from the broadcast's own standings graphics (decisive, no external source needed):**
   - Day-2 final standings (frame-verified): V1BA **0** WWCD; SOUL 1 WWCD.
   - Day-3 final standings (frame-verified): V1BA **2** WWCD; SOUL 2 WWCD.
   - Day-3 winners otherwise accounted for: M13 GDR, M14 SOUL, M15 K9, M16 WELT, M17 V1BA.
   - V1BA has 2 WWCD in the final and won M17 → the second WWCD can only be **M18**. SOUL has 2 in the final, had 1 after Day 2, won M14 → cannot have won M18. QED from the broadcast's own numbers.

3. **Broadcast-behavior evidence:** the VOD skips the M18 WWCD graphic entirely and cuts from the M18 endgame straight to Soul's trophy celebration — which is exactly what caused Analyst A's misread (understandable: the celebration footage is all Soul). Analyst B's frame @ t=14500 independently shows V1BA celebrating with "WINNER" behind during M17/M18 coverage.

**What actually happened on Day 3:** SOUL reached the smash point (126) after M14 and was on match point from M15. SOUL then **could not convert** — M15 K9, M16 WELT, M17 V1BA, M18 V1BA (Blue Army). No match-point-eligible team (SOUL, K9) won any of M16–M18, so the **Smash Rule fallback fired live**: "If no team completes both conditions by Match 18, the team with the most overall points wins" → **SOUL 155**, champions. The title was decided by fallback, not by conversion — a first-class broadcast beat AEGIS must implement (see Gap Audit, Smash fallback).
